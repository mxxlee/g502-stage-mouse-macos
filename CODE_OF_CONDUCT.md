# Code of conduct

Be respectful, patient, and specific. People contributing here may be video
editors, designers, hardware enthusiasts, first-time coders, or experienced
developers.

Constructive technical disagreement is welcome. Harassment, personal attacks,
discrimination, doxxing, and deliberately unsafe advice are not.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these rules.
Serious concerns can be reported privately through GitHub's security reporting
tools.
